<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Travel Guide is a travel-focused app where you pick a place on a map and see its details.</p>

<h2>Problem</h2>
<p>Travel content needed to be presented simply, in the context of a map.</p>

<h2>Solution</h2>
<p>The app brings destinations together in a readable experience where each one can be selected on the map.</p>

<h2>My Role</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Frontend development</li>
  <li>Content flow design</li>
</ul>

<h2>Key Features</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Place selection on the map</li>
  <li>Place details</li>
  <li>Simple travel flow</li>
</ul>

<h2>Technical Highlights</h2>
<ul>
  <li>React</li>
  <li>Leaflet</li>
  <li>API integration</li>
</ul>

<h2>Why It Matters</h2>
<p>Travel Guide makes travel content easier to understand by showing it in the context of a map.</p>
