<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Mars is a personal web page I built for my son: his very first web page.</p>

<h2>Problem</h2>
<p>Family and personal memories needed a simple space where they could be gathered on a single page.</p>

<h2>Solution</h2>
<p>The page brings together a countdown to his birth, family and playmate photos, and a flow for leaving a message for Mars.</p>

</ul>

  <li>Message sending flow</li>
</ul>

  <li>CSS</li>
</ul>
