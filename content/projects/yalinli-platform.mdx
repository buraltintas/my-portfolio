<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Yalınlı Platform is a local civic platform where Yalınlı residents can report issues, share suggestions, and follow community updates. It is a real-world civic-tech product built for community reporting and local impact.</p>

<h2>Problem</h2>
<p>Local problems, suggestions, events, and community announcements needed to be collected in an organized, structured way.</p>

<h2>Solution</h2>
<p>The platform brings public submissions, anonymous reporting, multilingual content, and petition flows together in one place. Residents can sign petitions, and reports can be exported.</p>
