<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Racing Cars is a simple racing game where you pick a Formula 1 car and race against three random cars. It is built around a racing-themed interface and quick visual feedback.</p>

<h2>Problem</h2>
<p>The goal was to deliver a racing theme in a simple web experience.</p>

<h2>Solution</h2>
<p>The project offers a focused experience: you choose a Formula 1 car and go through a short race flow.</p>
