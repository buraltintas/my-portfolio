<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Racing Cars is a simple browser game. You pick a Formula 1 car and race against three random cars, with quick visual feedback along the way.</p>

<h2>Problem</h2>
<p>I wanted to turn a racing theme into a small game that is quick to play.</p>

<h2>Solution</h2>
<p>The game is kept short: you choose your Formula 1 car and the race against the other three begins.</p>
