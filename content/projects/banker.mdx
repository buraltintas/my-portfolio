<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Bankacı is an app for bankers who price loans and for people looking for one. Both run on the same calculation engine. The banker walks into the meeting prepared and sends the result to the customer in one tap; the borrower knows what they will pay before they sign.</p>
<p>The product has four parts: the iOS and Android app, bankaci.app, a web portal on bankaci.app, and KrediBul (kredibul.bankaci.app), which connects borrowers with bankers. The app has been on the App Store since February 2025.</p>

<h2>Problem</h2>
<p>In a branch, loan figures are usually worked out on the bank's own screen. Comparing payment plans, checking the legal limits and sending the customer a proper document are separate jobs. On the borrower's side, the instalment, the taxes and the total cost often stay unclear until the signature.</p>

<h2>What It Does</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Eight calculators:</strong> personal (consumer, vehicle, housing), business (instalment, spot, revolving, cheque and bill discounting), mortgage refinancing, how much you can borrow for a given instalment, overdraft and credit card, deposit, early repayment, and savings finance.</li>
  <li><strong>Seven payment plans:</strong> standard, prepaid interest, equal principal, custom/balloon, grace period, increasing and decreasing instalments. KKDF and BSMV taxes are included.</li>
  <li><strong>Legal limits:</strong> the banking regulator's (BDDK) term and loan-to-value limits and the central bank's (TCMB) maximum overdraft and credit card rates appear in the calculation, each with its legal basis.</li>
  <li><strong>Car value for vehicle loans:</strong> the car is picked from the TSB kasko (insurance value) list, and the legal loan and term limits follow from its value.</li>
  <li><strong>Rates the banks publish:</strong> the banks' own pages are read once a week and the rates are shown with their source. Tapping a rate opens the calculator with it, and a result is compared with the banks' rates.</li>
  <li><strong>PDF and sharing:</strong> results go out as a PDF or an image. A banker's PDF ends with their request link and QR code; without a link, and with contact details added, it ends with a WhatsApp QR.</li>
  <li><strong>For bankers:</strong> a permanent request link; one inbox for leads from the link, the pool and campaigns; the KrediBul pool; and publishing their own campaigns.</li>
  <li><strong>For borrowers:</strong> one request on KrediBul and applications to bankers' campaigns.</li>
  <li><strong>AI Assistant:</strong> describe the case in a sentence and the assistant suggests the right calculator and plan. Every number comes from the app's own calculation engine, not from the model.</li>
  <li><strong>Siri:</strong> say "Calculate a loan with Bankacı" (in Turkish, "Bankacı ile kredi hesapla") and Siri asks for the amount, term and rate, then reads out the monthly instalment and the total repayment (iOS 17 and later).</li>
  <li><strong>Home screen widget</strong> on iOS and Android, a <strong>weekly rates email</strong>, notifications, Turkish and English.</li>
  <li><strong>Premium:</strong> monthly, yearly and lifetime plans, with a 7-day free trial on monthly and yearly.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Technical Highlights</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Mobile:</strong> Expo, React Native and TypeScript. Swift App Intents for Siri, WidgetKit and SwiftUI for the iOS widget, Kotlin for the Android widget and in-app updates. The calculations are covered by Jest tests.</li>
  <li><strong>One calculation engine:</strong> the TypeScript calculation code is the same in the app and the web portal, so both give the same numbers. Siri runs the same engine inside JavaScriptCore instead of a Swift copy.</li>
  <li><strong>Backend:</strong> Go with only the standard library's net/http for HTTP, and PostgreSQL (Neon). Sign-in with an email code.</li>
  <li><strong>Web:</strong> bankaci.app and KrediBul are server-rendered Next.js. Every page exists in Turkish and English, with structured data, a sitemap and llms.txt. The web portal is React with Vite.</li>
  <li><strong>Infrastructure:</strong> Google Cloud Run, Cloud Build with separate test and production environments, Cloud Scheduler, and a database backup that is restored and checked every day.</li>
  <li><strong>Reading bank rates:</strong> page text goes to OpenAI with a strict JSON schema; pages that draw their rates with JavaScript go through a separate headless Chromium service.</li>
  <li><strong>Other services:</strong> RevenueCat for subscriptions, Expo Push for notifications, Resend for email, AdMob for ads.</li>
  <li><strong>Privacy:</strong> calculations run on the device. Analytics are not tied to the account and carry no name, phone number or email.</li>
</ul>
