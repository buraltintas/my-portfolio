<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Banker is a credit calculation and sharing app for bankers and finance professionals who need fast, reliable estimates during client conversations.</p>

<h2>Problem</h2>
<p>Credit calculations are often done in fragmented tools or spreadsheets, which slows down client conversations and makes sharing results harder than it should be.</p>

<h2>Solution</h2>
<p>Banker provides a focused mobile workflow for calculating credit terms quickly and sharing the result in a professional format without leaving the app.</p>

<h2>My Role</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Product idea and UX direction</li>
  <li>React Native frontend development</li>
  <li>Share flow implementation</li>
  <li>Web deployment and mobile release preparation</li>
  <li>App Store and Google Play publishing</li>
</ul>

<h2>Key Features</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Fast credit calculation</li>
  <li>Shareable result output</li>
  <li>Clean mobile-first interface</li>
  <li>Professional use case for client meetings</li>
</ul>
