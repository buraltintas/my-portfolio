<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>DomainDock is a privacy-first Chrome extension for taking domain-based notes, with local-only storage.</p>

<h2>Problem</h2>
<p>Organizing notes by context inside the browser, while keeping your data under your own control, is often hard.</p>

<h2>Solution</h2>
<p>DomainDock ties each note to the relevant domain and offers a local-first workflow.</p>

<h2>My Role</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Product idea and UX direction</li>
  <li>Chrome extension development</li>
  <li>Storage and export/import flows</li>
</ul>

<h2>Key Features</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Domain-based notes</li>
  <li>Local-only storage</li>
  <li>Export/import support</li>
  <li>Clean, productivity-focused interface</li>
</ul>

<h2>Why It Matters</h2>
<p>DomainDock makes contextual note-taking more practical without compromising on privacy.</p>
