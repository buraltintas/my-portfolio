<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Talk Tracker is a small web tool for keeping track of which participants have spoken in a meeting. It is built with Next.js.</p>

<h2>Problem</h2>
<p>In meetings, it is easy to lose track of who has already contributed and who has not spoken yet.</p>

<h2>Solution</h2>
<p>The facilitator adds the participants before the meeting and marks each one with a click as they speak. Meetings are saved in the browser's localStorage, so earlier meetings stay in a list. There is no account and no backend.</p>

<h2>My Role</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Product idea and UX direction</li>
  <li>Frontend development</li>
  <li>Interaction design for quick toggling</li>
  <li>End-to-end tests with Cypress</li>
</ul>

<h2>Key Features</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Participant list creation</li>
  <li>Speaking status tracking</li>
  <li>Saved meetings list</li>
  <li>No sign-up required</li>
</ul>

<h2>Technical Highlights</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Next.js 13.4 with the pages router</li>
  <li>React and JavaScript</li>
  <li>CSS modules</li>
  <li>localStorage for saved meetings</li>
  <li>Cypress end-to-end tests</li>
</ul>

<h2>Why It Matters</h2>
<p>During a meeting, you can see at a glance who has not spoken yet.</p>
