<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Talk Tracker is a lightweight meeting tool for tracking which participants have spoken during a session.</p>

<h2>Problem</h2>
<p>In meetings, it is easy to lose track of who has already contributed and who still has not spoken.</p>

<h2>Solution</h2>
<p>Talk Tracker gives facilitators a simple way to add participants before a meeting and mark them as they speak.</p>

<h2>My Role</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Product idea and UX direction</li>
  <li>Frontend development</li>
  <li>Interaction design for quick toggling</li>
</ul>

<h2>Key Features</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Participant list creation</li>
  <li>Speaking status tracking</li>
  <li>Instant meeting use</li>
  <li>No sign-up required</li>
</ul>

<h2>Technical Highlights</h2>
<ul>
  <li>React</li>
  <li>JavaScript</li>
  <li>CSS</li>
  <li>Lightweight web app structure</li>
</ul>

<h2>Why It Matters</h2>
<p>Talk Tracker solves a small but real meeting problem with a focused interface that makes participation easier to follow.</p>
