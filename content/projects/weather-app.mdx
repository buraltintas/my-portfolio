<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Weather App is a small project: enter a city name and see the weather forecast for it.</p>

<h2>Problem</h2>
<p>Checking the forecast for a city should take a few seconds.</p>

<h2>Solution</h2>
<p>The app gets the forecast from a weather API and shows the main details on one screen.</p>
