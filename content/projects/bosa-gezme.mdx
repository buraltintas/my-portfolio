<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Boşa Gezme! is a location-aware discovery platform for real physical home and living stores.</p>

<h2>The Problem</h2>
<p>Online search often answers “What can I buy?” Boşa Gezme! focuses on “Which physical store is actually worth visiting?” It brings store information, community experiences, and location context together to make that decision easier.</p>

<h2>Product Loop</h2>
<p>Discover &rarr; Visit &rarr; Review &rarr; Help the next person discover</p>

<h2>My Role</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Product idea and UX direction</li>
  <li>Frontend and backend development</li>
  <li>Location-aware discovery and review flows</li>
  <li>Authentication and multilingual product structure</li>
  <li>Google and OpenAI integrations</li>
  <li>Production setup and deployment</li>
</ul>

<h2>Key Product Capabilities</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Discovery of real physical stores</li>
  <li>Location-aware search</li>
  <li>Community reviews and ratings</li>
  <li>Store detail pages</li>
  <li>Posts, comments, likes, follows, and favorites</li>
  <li>Visit and proximity-aware review flow</li>
  <li>Turkish, English, German, and Russian product experience</li>
  <li>Anonymous browsing</li>
  <li>Google and email OTP authentication</li>
</ul>

<h2>Engineering</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Next.js, React, and TypeScript frontend</li>
  <li>Browser &rarr; Next.js BFF &rarr; Go API architecture</li>
  <li>Modular Go backend with a REST API</li>
  <li>PostgreSQL and PostGIS for location-aware data</li>
  <li>Google Cloud Run and Cloud Storage infrastructure</li>
  <li>Google Cloud Secret Manager</li>
  <li>Google Identity Services, Google Places, Gmail API, and OpenAI integrations</li>
  <li>Google-provided store data kept visibly separate from Boşa Gezme! community data</li>
</ul>

<h2>Product Philosophy</h2>
<p>Boşa Gezme! is built around physical-world usefulness: reducing wasted trips, keeping data sources clear, and bringing community experiences into a simple discover &rarr; visit &rarr; review loop.</p>
