<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>My Super Notes is a note-taking app with a simple interface. You sign in with your Google account and your notes stay in one place.</p>

<h2>Problem</h2>
<p>Quick notes tend to end up scattered in different places.</p>

<h2>Solution</h2>
<p>The app lets you write and edit your notes in one plain interface, tied to your Google account.</p>

<h2>My Role</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Frontend development</li>
  <li>Note layout design</li>
  <li>Google sign-in flow</li>
</ul>

<h2>Key Features</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Quick note-taking</li>
  <li>Editing saved notes</li>
  <li>Sign in with Google</li>
</ul>

<h2>Technical Highlights</h2>
<ul>
  <li>React</li>
  <li>Firebase</li>
  <li>Google Auth</li>
</ul>

<h2>Why It Matters</h2>
<p>It puts Google sign-in and Firebase to use in a small tool for everyday notes.</p>
