<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>My Super Notes is a fast note-taking app that keeps personal notes organized in a simple interface. You sign in with your Google account.</p>

<h2>Problem</h2>
<p>Quickly capturing notes and keeping them in one place can often turn into a scattered experience.</p>

<h2>Solution</h2>
<p>The app combines taking and editing notes in one clean interface.</p>

<h2>My Role</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Frontend development</li>
  <li>Note layout design</li>
  <li>Google sign-in flow</li>
</ul>

<h2>Key Features</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Fast note-taking</li>
  <li>Organized content structure</li>
  <li>Sign in with Google</li>
</ul>

<h2>Technical Highlights</h2>
<ul>
  <li>React</li>
  <li>Firebase</li>
  <li>Google Auth</li>
</ul>

<h2>Why It Matters</h2>
<p>My Super Notes makes everyday note-taking faster and more accessible.</p>
