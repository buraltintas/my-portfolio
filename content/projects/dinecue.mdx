<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>
  DineCue helps people make better dining decisions by combining user intent,
  taste preferences, location context, restaurant data, and AI-generated
  reasoning.
</p>

<h2>My Role</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Product idea and UX direction</li>
  <li>Frontend development</li>
  <li>Backend and API architecture</li>
  <li>Authentication flow</li>
  <li>AI and Google Places integration</li>
  <li>Deployment and production setup</li>
  <li>Domain, DNS, and email setup</li>
  <li>SEO and multilingual structure</li>
</ul>

<h2>Problem</h2>
<p>
  Choosing where to eat is often harder than it should be, especially while
  traveling or comparing unfamiliar restaurants and menus. Generic ratings do
  not always explain whether a place actually fits the user&apos;s taste,
  context, budget, or situation.
</p>

<h2>Solution</h2>
<p>
  DineCue turns dining context into personalized recommendations. Users can
  describe what they want, provide location or restaurant/menu context, and
  receive suggestions with reasoning, cautions, what to order, and why a place
  fits.
</p>

<h2>Key Features</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Email OTP login</li>
  <li>Google Sign-In</li>
  <li>Personalized restaurant and dining recommendations</li>
  <li>Google Places integration</li>
  <li>AI-generated recommendation reasoning</li>

  <li>Monthly free usage quota</li>
