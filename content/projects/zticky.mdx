<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Zticky is a mobile app that turns photos and videos into WhatsApp sticker packs. It removes the background on the phone, and you can add an outline, a shape or a few words. AI can restyle a sticker as a cartoon or anime, or follow a plain request such as "add sunglasses". A video can become an animated sticker too. There is no account, and the photos stay on the phone.</p>
<p>The iPhone and Android apps are coming soon, in 13 languages. The <a href="/zticky">support page</a> also links to the privacy policy and terms.</p>

<h2>Technical Highlights</h2>
<p>The app is built with React Native, Expo and TypeScript, with Skia for drawing. Background removal runs on the device, with Apple's Vision framework on iPhone and ML Kit on Android. For AI styles, a small server on Cloudflare Workers and D1 calls Google's Gemini image model. Subscriptions run through RevenueCat and ads through AdMob.</p>
