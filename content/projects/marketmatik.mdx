<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>MarketMatik is a mobile grocery shopping app that helps users track spending while building a shopping list.</p>

<h2>Problem</h2>
<p>People often lose track of their grocery budget while shopping and only notice the total when they reach the cashier.</p>

<h2>Solution</h2>
<p>MarketMatik lets users create a list, set a target amount, and enter prices while shopping so they can see their running total immediately.</p>

<h2>My Role</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Product idea and UX direction</li>
  <li>React Native mobile development</li>
  <li>Budget tracking flow design</li>
  <li>Web deployment and mobile release preparation</li>
  <li>App Store and Google Play publishing</li>
</ul>

<h2>Key Features</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Grocery list creation</li>
  <li>Target amount tracking</li>
  <li>Running total updates</li>
  <li>In-store use with a simple UI</li>
</ul>

<h2>Technical Highlights</h2>

  <li>TypeScript</li>
  <li>iOS and Android support</li>
</ul>

<h2>Why It Matters</h2>
<p>MarketMatik turns a common shopping frustration into a focused utility that helps users stay within budget in real time.</p>
