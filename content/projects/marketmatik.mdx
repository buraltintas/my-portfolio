<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>MarketMatik is a grocery list app for iOS and Android that keeps track of what you spend while you shop.</p>

<h2>Problem</h2>
<p>People often lose track of their grocery budget while shopping and only see the total at the checkout.</p>

<h2>Solution</h2>
<p>Users create a list, set a target amount and enter each price as they shop, so the running total is always on screen.</p>

<h2>My Role</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Product idea and UX direction</li>
  <li>React Native mobile development</li>
  <li>Budget tracking flow design</li>
  <li>Mobile release preparation</li>
  <li>App Store and Google Play publishing</li>
</ul>

<h2>Key Features</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Grocery list creation</li>
  <li>Target amount tracking</li>
  <li>Running total updates</li>
  <li>A simple interface for use in the store</li>
</ul>

<h2>Technical Highlights</h2>
<ul>
  <li>React Native</li>
  <li>Expo</li>
  <li>JavaScript</li>
  <li>iOS and Android support</li>
</ul>

<h2>Why It Matters</h2>
<p>Seeing the total before you reach the cashier makes it easier to stay within budget.</p>
