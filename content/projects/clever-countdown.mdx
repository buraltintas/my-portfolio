<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Clever Countdown is a <a href="https://www.youtube.com/@CleverCountdown" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">YouTube channel</a> that posts a two-minute "on this day in history" video every day. Each episode takes one calendar date and tells its inventions, firsts, famous births and turning points with real archive footage and photos. The first episodes went out in November 2026.</p>
<p>I built the AI video studio that makes the episodes. It runs on my own computer and is private; only the channel is public.</p>

<h2>How It Works</h2>
<p>The studio researches the date and checks the facts, writes and narrates the script, finds licensed footage and edits the video. Once someone on the team approves an episode, it goes up to YouTube with captions in ten languages.</p>

<h2>Technical Highlights</h2>
<p>The studio is a Next.js and TypeScript app with a SQLite database. Remotion and ffmpeg render the video, Claude does the research and writing, and ElevenLabs reads the narration. Uploads go through the YouTube Data API.</p>
