<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Recipe Book is an app that presents recipes in an organized, easy-to-browse structure.</p>

<h2>Problem</h2>
<p>A simple structure was needed for adding, viewing, and searching recipes all in one place.</p>

<h2>Solution</h2>
<p>The app presents recipes in a book-like layout with search support.</p>
