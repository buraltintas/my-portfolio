<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Coffee Dictionary is a modern coffee glossary that brings coffee knowledge together in one place, with daily featured terms, detailed explanations, and curated events.</p>

<h2>Problem</h2>
<p>Coffee terminology is often scattered across different sources and can be hard for newcomers to access.</p>

<h2>Solution</h2>
<p>The app combines the dictionary format with daily content and events to make learning easier.</p>
