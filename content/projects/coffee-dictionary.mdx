<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Coffee Dictionary is a coffee glossary with more than 250 terms in English, German and Turkish. Home shows a term of the day and the terms people look up most, and each term page links to related terms.</p>

<h2>Problem</h2>
<p>Coffee terminology is scattered across many sources, and newcomers have a hard time finding a clear definition.</p>

<h2>Solution</h2>
<p>The app pairs the glossary with one new term every day. Signed-in users can get it as a push notification or by email, and notifications wait out the quiet hours each user sets. Sign-in uses a one-time code sent by email, so there is no password. The app has no ads, no tracking and no in-app purchases.</p>
<p>Editors keep the glossary growing. People can apply from their profile, or an admin can invite them. Once approved, they propose and edit terms and improve translations from inside the app.</p>
