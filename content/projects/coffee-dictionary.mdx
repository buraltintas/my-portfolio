<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Coffee Dictionary is a modern coffee glossary that brings coffee knowledge together in one place, in three languages, with a term of the day, detailed explanations, and the terms people look up most.</p>

<h2>Problem</h2>
<p>Coffee terminology is often scattered across different sources and can be hard for newcomers to access.</p>

<h2>Solution</h2>
<p>The app combines the dictionary format with daily content to make learning easier. The glossary grows with a community of editors: people apply from their profile, and once approved they can propose and maintain terms and improve translations from inside the app.</p>

<h2>My Role</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Product idea and content direction</li>
  <li>Frontend development</li>
  <li>Backend and data layer development</li>
  <li>Deployment and production setup</li>
  <li>App Store and Google Play publishing</li>
</ul>

<h2>Key Features</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Term of the day</li>
  <li>Detailed definitions</li>
  <li>Most looked-up terms</li>
  <li>Three languages</li>
  <li>Editor program: apply, get reviewed, and editor tools appear in the app once approved</li>
  <li>A simple experience designed for mobile use</li>
</ul>

<h2>Technical Highlights</h2>
<ul>
  <li>React Native</li>
  <li>Expo</li>
  <li>TypeScript</li>
  <li>Go API, PostgreSQL and Cloud Run</li>
  <li>Next.js website and admin panel</li>
  <li>Role-based access: editors manage terms and translations, while destructive actions and user administration stay with admins</li>
</ul>

<h2>Why It Matters</h2>
<p>Coffee Dictionary turns a niche field into an organized, accessible product experience.</p>
