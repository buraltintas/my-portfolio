<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Voting App is a small polling tool that lets users create a vote, share the link, and watch results update live.</p>

<h2>Problem</h2>
<p>Teams and groups often need a quick way to run polls without dealing with heavy survey software or onboarding steps.</p>

<h2>Solution</h2>
<p>Voting App lets users create up to six options, share a voting link, and follow the results in real time.</p>

<h2>My Role</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Product idea and UX direction</li>
  <li>React frontend development</li>
  <li>Firebase data setup</li>
  <li>Live results flow implementation</li>
</ul>

<h2>Key Features</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Poll creation with up to 6 options</li>
  <li>Shareable voting links</li>
  <li>Live vote updates</li>
  <li>No sign-up required</li>
</ul>

<h2>Technical Highlights</h2>
<ul>
  <li>React</li>
  <li>Firebase</li>
  <li>Realtime Database</li>
  <li>Netlify deployment</li>
</ul>

<h2>Why It Matters</h2>
<p>Voting App packages a common polling workflow into a simple real-time product that people can use immediately.</p>
