<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Voting App is a small polling tool: create a vote, share the link and watch the results update live.</p>

<h2>Problem</h2>
<p>Teams and groups often need a quick poll without heavy survey software or a sign-up step.</p>

<h2>Solution</h2>
<p>Users create a poll with up to six options and share its voting link. Votes are stored in Cloud Firestore, and the results page listens to them with <code>onSnapshot</code>, so the counts change as people vote.</p>

<h2>My Role</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Product idea and UX direction</li>
  <li>Next.js frontend development</li>
  <li>Cloud Firestore data setup</li>
  <li>Live results flow implementation</li>
</ul>

<h2>Key Features</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Poll creation with up to 6 options</li>
  <li>Shareable voting links</li>
  <li>Live vote updates</li>
  <li>No sign-up required</li>
</ul>

<h2>Technical Highlights</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Next.js 13.2 and React</li>
  <li>Firebase</li>
  <li>Cloud Firestore with real-time listeners</li>
  <li>Netlify deployment</li>
</ul>

<h2>Why It Matters</h2>
<p>A group can run a poll and see the results right away, without anyone creating an account.</p>
