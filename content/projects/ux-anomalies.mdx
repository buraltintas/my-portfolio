<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>UX Anomalies was a platform designed to collect unusual UX problems, broken flows, and product inconsistencies from real digital products. It worked like an Instagram for UX oddities: a full-stack product for documenting real-world UX problems.</p>

<h2>Problem</h2>
<p>The strange user experiences we run into in everyday products were hard to document in one place, in a clear and organized way.</p>

<h2>Solution</h2>
<p>The platform made it easy to share UX examples, discover them, and make them visible through SEO-friendly pages. Users signed in, uploaded images of what they found, and liked and commented on each other's posts.</p>

<h2>My Role</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Product idea and design direction</li>
  <li>Frontend development</li>
  <li>Backend and data layer development</li>
  <li>Deployment and production setup</li>
</ul>

<h2>Key Features</h2>
<ul>
  <li>UX sharing with image uploads</li>
  <li>Content discovery</li>
  <li>Likes and comments</li>
  <li>SEO-friendly public pages with sitemap support</li>
</ul>

<h2>Technical Highlights</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Next.js frontend</li>
  <li>Go backend</li>

  <li>Neon PostgreSQL and Google Cloud Storage</li>
  <li>Authentication and image uploads</li>
</ul>

<h2>Why It Matters</h2>
