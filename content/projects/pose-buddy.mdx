<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Pose Buddy is a portrait-first mobile puzzle game prototype built with Phaser, TypeScript, and Capacitor. Players adjust a 9-joint stickman to match target pose cards, with similarity scoring, level progression, and a mobile-first touch interaction model.</p>

<h2>Problem</h2>
<p>The goal was to create a fast, touch-focused pose-matching experience on mobile.</p>

<h2>Solution</h2>
<p>Players adjust the 9-joint stickman's pose to match the target cards, earning points and advancing through levels.</p>
