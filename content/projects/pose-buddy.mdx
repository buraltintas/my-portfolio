<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Pose Buddy is a portrait-first puzzle game for iPhone, built with Phaser, TypeScript and Capacitor. Version 1.0 has been on the App Store since March 28, 2026. Players move a stickman with nine joints to match a target pose card, and the game scores how close they get.</p>

<h2>Problem</h2>
<p>I wanted a pose-matching game that felt quick and natural with touch controls on a phone.</p>

<h2>Solution</h2>
<p>Each level shows a target card. Players drag the stickman's joints until the shapes match, get a similarity score and unlock the next level. The History gallery lists solved levels with their best scores and lets players replay them, and the Pose Library shows the target poses as silhouettes.</p>
<p>The game is available in English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French and Portuguese. Optional reminder notifications are scheduled locally on the phone and are off by default. AdMob interstitial ads show only after a finished level, never during play.</p>
